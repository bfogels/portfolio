ABOUT

About me

I am a robotics, AI, and software engineer based in Baltimore, Maryland. My work spans medical devices, autonomous systems, machine learning, and community-focused engineering.

Current Focus

I currently work at Key Technologies on software for medical-device prototyping, embedded systems, and AI tooling. Earlier work included autonomy and engineering-operations software for unmanned maritime systems at Weather Gage Technologies.

Education

I earned an M.S. in Robotics from the University of Michigan in December 2020, focusing on AI, ML, controls, and human-centered systems.

I graduated from Johns Hopkins University in May 2019 after double majoring in Electrical Engineering and Computer Engineering, with minors in Entrepreneurship and Management, Robotics, and Computer Science.

Community Work

Community work is a major part of my engineering identity. I contributed to IEEE Standard 1872.1, mentor and judge for FRC Team 4541, taught ENG100 at Michigan, and led the HKN / IEEE student chapter at Hopkins.

Engineering Interests

I enjoy building useful software, especially at the intersection of robotics, AI, and embedded systems. Many of my favorite projects involve helping people directly through assistive technology, education, or tools that make complex systems more usable.
